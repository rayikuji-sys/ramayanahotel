# Content verification register

The public publishing gate is implemented, but several hotel facts still need an owner and a source before they should be added or marked verified in the staff workspace.

| Area | Verify before publishing |
| --- | --- |
| Rooms | Exact bed types, occupancy, room sizes, amenities, accessibility details, connecting rooms, and current room count by category |
| Rates | Current public rates, taxes, inclusions, blackout dates, and whether any offer is still active |
| Events | Venue assignment, floor, capacity by layout, minimum pax, menu inclusions, validity dates, and package pricing |
| Dining | Arjuna Resto and Rama Shinta Lounge opening hours, menus, dietary options, service periods, and reservation process |
| Services | Room service hours, laundry turnaround, transport coverage, gym access rules, and parking limitations |
| Nearby | Distances, travel times, operating hours, ticketing, and current names for every point of interest |
| Trust marks | HALAL, CHSE, Laik Sehat, and GoGreen status, issuing body, certificate number, and expiry |
| Partnerships | Healthcare partner identity, address, service scope, and current agreement |
| Social proof | Guest quote consent, attribution, date, and source URL |
| Contact | Telephone, WhatsApp routing, email inbox, Instagram URL, and response ownership |

Seeded event packages are kept informational and contact-led. Do not add a numeric price to public content until the hotel approves the amount and validity period.
