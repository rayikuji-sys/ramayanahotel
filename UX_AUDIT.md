# UX audit

The site is designed around a clear first action: contact the hotel with dates, guests, or event details. Navigation stays visible on desktop, becomes a compact mobile menu, and keeps a bottom mobile contact bar available for the main conversion action.

The public experience includes skip navigation, labeled form fields, visible focus states, keyboard-closeable dialogs, dialog focus restoration, reduced-motion handling, alt text for supplied photography, semantic headings, and no horizontal overflow at the tested mobile width.

The copy deliberately explains that a request is not a confirmed booking. This prevents a guest from mistaking a contact form for live inventory or a payment transaction.

Recommended next review with hotel staff:

- Confirm the preferred WhatsApp destination and who owns each inquiry during business hours.
- Test the event builder with real wedding, meeting, and private-business scenarios.
- Review every photo crop on a phone and replace any image whose subject is unclear.
- Add approved accessibility information, including step-free routes, lift access, and accessible room details.
- Validate the map pin, travel times, and nearby-place copy before publishing as guest guidance.
