# Visual refinement — 25 September 2026

References were studied for interaction principles, not copied as website assets or source code.

- [Codrops: Mastering Carousels with GSAP](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/04/21/mastering-carousels-with-gsap-from-basics-to-advanced-animation/): layered depth, animated scene transitions, responsive navigation. Implemented here with React state, native scrolling, CSS transforms, keyboard controls and reduced-motion support; no additional animation dependency.
- [Codrops: Fullscreen Image Slideshow Animations](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/09/27/some-ideas-for-fullscreen-image-slideshow-animations/): restrained image/copy transitions and full-bleed scenes. Facilities keep their established desktop arc and become a swipeable scene plus thumbnail rail on mobile.
- [Four Seasons Meetings & Events](https://www.fourseasons.com/meetings-and-events/): put occasion, spaces, capacity and planning needs ahead of the inquiry form. The Ramayana planner uses the hotel's supplied figures and original seating diagrams.
- [Aman Celebrations & Events](https://www.aman.com/celebrations-events): generous photographic layouts and a personal inquiry path.

The shared visual materials are white, warm ivory, champagne and charcoal. Glass is used for overlapping information panels and navigation; photography retains its natural colour. Hero video fills the viewport with cover sizing as requested. It retains the complete 8.04-second timeline; portrait views crop the sides to fill the screen.

The planning capacities in `lib/event-guide.ts` come from the supplied overview brochure (`2.png`) and seating diagrams (`3.png`, `4.png`, `5.png`). The latter are copied unchanged to `public/media/venue-*.png`. Conflicting figures are resolved conservatively for guidance and disclosed next to the comparison. Current rates and the final room arrangement must come from the hotel team.
